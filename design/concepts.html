Every concept uses your actual last 12 months, avatar and username. Each one is drawn inside the layout Apple Wallet really allows, so what you pick here is what you'd get on the phone. New: #17–31 lean harder on your photo and handle.

What holds for every concept

  • Text in the system font (top row and field rows) is real Wallet text: it updates, is readable by VoiceOver, and can't use custom fonts.
  • Anything in the banner or poster art is an image we render on the server: custom fonts, your avatar, charts. It updates daily with the rest of the card.
  • Stats are computed from your real days (active days, longest streak, best day). Nothing is invented.
  • The QR is optional. It links to your GitHub profile. Without it, Wallet leaves the bottom of a store card empty.
